Automation 1

Missed-Call Textback

A local business misses a call, the caller phones the next company on Google, the job is gone. This automation texts the caller back before that happens. It's the best first sell on this list because the owner already feels the pain every single day.

Build it in GHL as one workflow:

# Workflow: missed-call-textback
trigger:  Call Status = no answer / busy / voicemail
action 1: Send SMS
  "Sorry we missed you at {{location.name}}!
   How can we help? Book here: {{booking_link}}"
action 2: If/Else → contact replies
  yes → notify owner + push booking link
  no  → follow-up SMS after 1 hour
✓ lead answered while they're still holding the phone

Test it yourself: call the tracked number, let it ring out, and confirm the text lands within seconds. Then show the owner that test on their own phone. It closes deals on the spot.

Charge: $297/mo. One recovered job usually covers the whole month, and you should say exactly that in the pitch.
Automation 2

Review Engine

Owners beg for reviews more than anything else. The fix is timing: ask a few hours after the invoice is paid, while the customer is still happy, with a link that takes one tap.

# Workflow: review-request
trigger:  Invoice Paid  (or job marked complete in their CRM)
wait:     4 hours
action 1: Send SMS
  "Thanks for choosing {{location.name}}!
   Quick favor? One tap: {{review_link}}"
action 2: no click in 3 days → one polite nudge, then stop
✓ direct Google review link: g.page/r/YOUR-CLIENT/review

Get the real link from the client's Google Business Profile: hit "Ask for reviews" and copy the short URL. If a customer replies with a complaint instead of clicking, route that conversation to the owner immediately so they can fix it before it goes public.

Charge: $250/mo. Easiest pitch on this list because every owner already knows they need reviews.
Automation 3

Speed to Lead

Most local businesses take hours or days to answer a new lead. The first company to respond usually wins the job. Your automation answers in under a minute, every time, from every lead source.

# Workflow: speed-to-lead
trigger:  Form Submitted  (FB lead form, website, GMB chat)
action 1: Send SMS within 60s
  "Hi {{contact.first_name}}, saw your request!
   Want a quick quote today? Reply YES."
action 2: Send intro email + tag contact "hot-lead"
action 3: reply detected → Call Connect
  dials the owner's cell, then bridges the lead in
✓ lead talking to a human while competitors sleep

Connect every lead source the client has: Facebook lead forms plug into GHL natively, website forms embed directly, and anything else comes in through a webhook. The pitch writes itself: fill out their own contact form in front of them, then watch their face when the text arrives before you finish the sentence.

Charge: $500 setup + $400/mo. Setup fee is justified because wiring up lead sources is real work.
Automation 4

Appointment Reminders

No-shows quietly burn 20 to 30 percent of revenue in appointment businesses like med spas, salons, dentists, and detailers. Three timed reminders fix most of it, and the build takes an afternoon.

# Workflow: appointment-reminders
trigger:  Appointment Booked
action 1: SMS 24h before → "Reply C to confirm"
action 2: SMS 2h before  → address + parking note
action 3: SMS 30m before → "See you soon!"
branch:   reply "R" → send reschedule link
          a reschedule is a save, not a loss
✓ every appointment confirmed or rebooked, zero manual work

Use GHL's calendar or sync the client's existing Google Calendar so bookings flow in automatically. The reschedule branch matters most: a customer who reschedules was a guaranteed no-show a minute earlier.

Charge: $200/mo. Cheapest on the list, easiest yes you'll hear, and a perfect add-on to any other automation here.
Automation 5

FAQ Chatbot

Half the calls a local business gets are the same ten questions: hours, pricing, service area, do you do X. A bot trained on their actual business answers those on the website and over SMS, then books the job. At 2am. On Sundays.

Use Claude to build the knowledge base, then load it into GHL's Conversation AI:

> claude "Read acmeplumbing.com and this list of
  their 15 most common customer questions. Write a
  FAQ knowledge base with exact answers, plus rules:
  if the visitor wants a quote or booking, send
  {{booking_link}}. If unsure, say a human will
  follow up and tag the conversation for the owner."
✓ paste into Conversation AI → deploy to webchat + SMS

The escalation rule is the whole game. A bot that admits what it doesn't know and hands off to a human keeps the owner's trust. A bot that guesses about pricing loses you the client.

Charge: $500 setup + $300/mo. Price the training work, not the software.
