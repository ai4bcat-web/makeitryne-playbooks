The DM script that books the call

Your leads don't fill out forms on their phone. They DM. Here's the exact sequence and follow-up timing that turns a comment into a booked call.

Why the form is losing you calls

Three things happen to most Instagram leads. The ad sends them to a website form, and operators don't fill out forms on a phone. Somebody answers the DM four hours later, after they already booked the guy who answered in four minutes. And nobody follows up a second time, even though most booked calls come on the second or third touch.

The fix is after the click, not in the ad. Run the ad to a comment keyword, let the automation reply in under a minute, ask two questions, drop the calendar link, then have a human follow up twice in 48 hours. That's the whole funnel.

The DM sequence (copy it into ManyChat)

Message 1, sent within 60 seconds of the comment

Hey [first name], saw your comment. Quick one so I send you the right thing: what kind of work do you do, and roughly how many jobs a week are you running right now?

One message, two questions. Not five. You're qualifying and you're warming them up at the same time.

Message 2, after they answer

Got it. That's exactly who this is built for. Grab 15 minutes here and I'll walk you through what it would look like for your shop: [calendar link] If none of those times work, tell me a day and I'll make it work.

The calendar link goes in the second message, never the first. The first message earns the reply; the second one cashes it in.

If they go quiet before answering message 1

No rush. When you get a second, just tell me what kind of work you do and I'll send it over.

The follow-up timing (a human, not the bot)

Automation gets you the first reply in under a minute. People book on the second or third touch. So two human follow-ups go out inside 48 hours of the comment, from you or whoever runs your office.

Set it up in 20 minutes

1
Pick one keyword for the video. Short, one word, all caps in the caption. "Comment CALLS."
2
In ManyChat, build one automation: trigger on a comment containing the keyword, send Message 1, wait for the reply, send Message 2 with your calendar link.
3
Put the two human follow-ups on your own calendar as recurring reminders at 5 pm and 10 am. Whoever answers the phone does them.
4
Track three numbers every week: cost per lead, how many leads booked a call, and cost per booked call. Judge the ad by the last one.
