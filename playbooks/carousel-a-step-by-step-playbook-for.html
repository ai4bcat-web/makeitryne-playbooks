A Make It Ryne Playbook

Zero Portfolio + 30 Days = First AI Client

The exact playbook I'd run starting from nothing. No portfolio, no audience, no case studies. Just proof, focus, and 30 days of doing the boring thing well.

What you'll have by day 30

  • One boring, profitable niche picked on evidence, not vibes
  • One working automation that fixes one expensive problem
  • A 90-second Loom that replaces an entire portfolio
  • A researched list of 37 businesses that visibly need you
  • An outreach system running 10 personal DMs a day
Step 1 · Days 1-3

Pick Boring

Cool niches are broke. Boring niches pay. Rich, boring businesses bleed money on problems a weekend build can fix, and nobody is pitching them because everyone is chasing SaaS and ecom.

Open Claude and score niches by automation pain. You want a niche where the problem is visible from the outside and expensive on the inside.

> claude "rank 10 local business niches by automation pain.
  For each: the most expensive recurring problem, roughly
  what it costs them, and whether I can see it from outside"

NICHE SCORECARD
med spas    no-shows eat 20% of revenue   ★★★★★
roofers     leads die in voicemail        ★★★★☆
law firms   intake takes 3 days           ★★★☆☆
skipped: saas + ecom (every agency is already there)
✓ picked: med spas, one city, one problem

Then commit: one niche, one city, one problem. Write it as a single sentence. Mine would be: "I fix no-shows for med spas in Austin." If you can't say it in one sentence, you haven't picked yet.

Tip: rich and boring beats cool and broke. Every time.
Step 2 · Days 4-7

Build One System

You are not building a portfolio. You are building proof. One system that fixes the one expensive problem you just picked. For med spas, that's a no-show recovery agent: when an appointment gets missed, the system chases it automatically.

Use Claude Code to scaffold the workflow, n8n to run it, and a free Calendly account as your fake booking system to test against.

> claude "build me an n8n workflow: no-show recovery agent.
  Trigger on a missed-appointment webhook from Calendly.
  Step 1: SMS within 5 min of the miss.
  Step 2: email with a rebooking link.
  Step 3: alert the front desk if no reply in 24h.
  Output the workflow JSON I can import into n8n."

✓ trigger: missed-appointment webhook
✓ sms within 5 min → rebooking email → 24h escalation

> npx n8n
import the JSON, wire your Twilio + email creds, hit test
✓ test run passed, 3/3 messages fired

Three evenings of work. Near-zero cost: n8n self-hosted is free, Calendly is free, Twilio trial credit covers your test texts. The only thing that matters is that it fires for real, end to end, while you watch.

Tip: one system. Resist the urge to build five. Five half-built demos close zero clients.
Step 3 · Days 8-10

The Loom Proof

Now turn the system into an asset. Record a 90-second Loom of your build catching a fake missed appointment in real time. No slides, no intro music, no pitch deck. One video replaces every case study you don't have.

Script it tight:

# 90 SECONDS, NO SLIDES
0:00  "this is a fake med spa i built"
0:15  cancel an appointment on screen
0:40  sms + email fire in real time, show your phone
1:10  "want this running on yours?"

Face cam on. Energy up. Zero pitch. You are showing, not selling. Re-record it until it feels effortless, because the version where you stumble through it will not get replies, and the version where you sound like you've done this a hundred times will.

Tip: the moment the SMS hits your phone on camera is the whole video. Make sure it's visible.
Step 4 · Days 11-14

Build the List: 37 Real Leads

Volume outreach is dead. Small list, deep research wins. Pull about 100 local businesses in your niche and city, then keep only the ones with the visible bleeding problem.

> claude "pull med spas in austin from google maps,
  enrich with apollo: owner name, email, instagram,
  review count. Output leads.csv"

✓ leads.csv: 100 rows with owner, email, ig, reviews

> claude "go through leads.csv. Flag every business with
  no online booking, or reviews mentioning missed appts
  and phone tag. Delete the rest."

✓ 37 flagged with the visible leak
the other 63? deleted. focus wins.

For each of the 37, write one line of specifics: what you saw, where you saw it. A review complaining nobody called back is worth more than any cold email template ever written.

Tip: their bad reviews are your pitch. Quote the problem back to them in their customers' words.
Step 5 · Days 15-22

10 DMs a Day

Now the reps. Ten personal messages a day to your list of 37, on whatever channel the owner actually uses. For med spas that's usually Instagram or email. Every message follows the same shape: their specific problem, your Loom, one low-friction question.

# THE MESSAGE (60 words max)
"Hey [name], saw [specific thing: the review, the
missing booking link, the voicemail-only phone line].

I built a system that texts no-shows within 5 minutes
and rebooks them automatically. Here's a 90-second
video of it working: [loom link]

Worth a look for [business name]?"

No pricing in the first message. No "just following up" spam. Follow up twice, three days apart, each time with something new: a second angle on their problem, or a one-line result from your test build. Track every send in a sheet: name, date, channel, reply.

Tip: 10 great messages beat 100 templated ones. If you can swap the name into another message unchanged, it's too generic.
Step 6 · Days 23-30

Close the First One

With 37 researched leads and real proof, expect a handful of replies. When someone bites, don't pitch a retainer. Offer a paid pilot: "I'll set this up on your calendar this week. Flat setup fee, and if it doesn't catch a single no-show in 30 days, I refund it." Price it low enough that saying yes is easy and high enough that they take it seriously.

You already built the system. Deployment is wiring your existing workflow into their booking tool, which is days, not weeks. Deliver fast, over-communicate, and ask for the referral the moment the first recovered appointment lands.

Client one is the hard one. Client two comes from client one's Loom.

The 30-Day Checklist

  1. Score niches by automation pain. Pick one niche, one city, one problem.
  2. Build one system that fixes that one problem. Claude Code + n8n + Calendly.
  3. Test it end to end until it fires 3/3.
  4. Record the 90-second Loom. Face cam on, zero pitch. Re-record until effortless.
  5. Pull 100 local leads, enrich them, keep the 37 with the visible leak.
  6. Send 10 personal DMs a day: their problem, your Loom, one question.
  7. Follow up twice, three days apart, with something new each time.
  8. Close the first reply with a low-risk paid pilot, deliver in days, ask for the referral.
