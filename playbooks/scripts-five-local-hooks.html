SCRIPTS · free playbook

Five hooks any local business can steal

All five hooks from the video, written as fill-in-the-blank scripts you can say for your business this week.

How to use this

1
Pick one hook. Not five. One a week.
2
Fill in the brackets with your trade, your town, your real price. Say it out loud once. If it sounds like an ad, cut words until it sounds like you.
3
The hook is the first sentence of the video. Under 12 words. Then deliver exactly what it promised.

The five scripts

1. The call-out

Name who it's for in the first three words. The right person stops. Everybody else scrolls, and that's fine.

If you [have this exact problem], watch this.
  • Plumber: If your water heater is popping, watch this.
  • Dentist: If your gums bleed when you floss, watch this.
  • Gym: If you quit every gym by March, watch this.

2. The question you hear every day

Whatever your front desk answers ten times a week is a video. Ask it, then answer it straight.

[The question customers always ask]? Here's the real answer.
  • Plumber: Do I really need to replace it, or can you fix it? Here's the real answer.
  • Dentist: How long does a crown actually take? Here's the real answer.
  • Gym: Is 24/7 access actually safe at 2 a.m.? Here's the real answer.

3. Behind the scenes

The part of your business customers never see. No script needed past the first line. Just film it.

Here's what [your shop / office / truck] looks like at [time nobody sees].
  • Plumber: Here's what my truck looks like at 6 a.m. before the first call.
  • Dentist: Here's what we do to a room between every patient.
  • Gym: Here's what our gym looks like at 5 a.m.

4. The price, before they ask

Everybody wants to know. Nobody else will say it. Say a real range from your own invoices.

What [your service] really costs at [your business]: [your price range]. Here's what changes it.
  • Plumber: What a water heater swap really costs: [your price range]. Here's what changes it.
  • Dentist: What a crown really costs without insurance: [your price range]. Here's what changes it.
  • Gym: What a membership here really costs: [your monthly price]. No sign-up fee games.

5. Before and after

Show the problem first. Hold it a second longer than feels comfortable. Then the fix.

This is what [the problem] looked like this morning. Here's after.
  • Plumber: This is what that drain line looked like this morning. Here's after.
  • Dentist: The smile before. Then after. (With the patient's written OK.)
  • Gym: This is what this corner looked like before we rebuilt it. Here's after.

Rules that keep them working

  • Real prices, real photos, real customers. If you don't have the number, don't make one up. Use a different hook that week.
  • Rotate. Five hooks, five weeks, then start over with new examples.
  • Write down which hook you used and how many views it got: [your number]. After five weeks you'll know which one your customers stop for.
Here's the deal. You don't need new ideas. You need one of these on a sticky note by the phone. Steal one a week and you're never stuck.
