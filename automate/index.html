The real problem

You are the bottleneck.

  • You film once, then lose three days cutting clips, writing captions, and designing carousels.
  • You post to one platform because repurposing to the rest is a second full-time job.
  • Good ideas die in your notes app because turning one into a finished post takes an afternoon.
  • When work gets busy, content stops. Then leads stop about three weeks later.

It is not an effort problem. You have plenty of effort. It is that every single step waits on you.

Questions

The things people ask.

Do I need to be technical?

No. I build it and hand it over running. You review a queue and approve what goes out. If you can use a phone and say yes or no, you can run it.

Will it sound like AI wrote it?

It writes against your style guide, your past posts and your actual phrasing, not a generic template. And you approve everything before it publishes, so anything off never sees daylight.

Do I still have to film?

Yes. The machine handles everything around the camera, not the camera. You are the reason people follow you, so replacing you is the one thing it should not do.

What happens when the tools change?

The system is built in pieces, so a model or platform swap is a replaced part rather than a rebuild. That is a design decision, and it is why it has survived the changes so far.

What if I want to stop working with you?

It keeps running. It lives on your accounts and your keys. That is deliberate, and it is the difference between owning a system and renting one.

How long until it is live?

Book the call and I will give you a real timeline for your setup instead of a number that sounds good on a landing page. It depends on how many platforms you run and what already exists.
